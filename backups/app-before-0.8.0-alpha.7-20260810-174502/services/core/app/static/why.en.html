Why Personal Agent

It is not just a window to a model. The product is designed to deliver verifiable user outcomes while keeping data, privacy and execution under explicit control.

Local-first

Local models and data can stay on your computer; infrastructure details are hidden from ordinary users.

Outcome, not a promise

Files, code and reports are considered complete only after verification.

Web with evidence

Fresh answers use retrieved sources and keep citations separate from the synthesized answer.

One workspace

Chats, projects, files, tasks and artifacts live in one product.

Flexible AI routing

Users choose simple modes while administrators configure providers and models.

Cost and access control

Local inference, remote quotas, roles and commercial policies are separate concerns.
